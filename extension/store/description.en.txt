SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 132 characters)

Every Austrian rental portal in one search. Duplicates merged, price drops tracked, all data kept private in your browser.


DETAILED DESCRIPTION

RentRadar Austria searches the big Austrian rental portals, housing cooperatives and small brokers at once, and turns the results into one clean list without duplicates.

Stop opening ten tabs every morning. Save your search once, press refresh, and see what's new.

ONE SEARCH, EVERY SOURCE
• Multiple sources scanned together: large portals, housing cooperatives and broker websites
• Apartments and houses: regular rentals, cooperative flats, council flats, flatshares and flat swaps
• Watch every source scan live, with results showing up as they arrive

NO MORE DUPLICATES
• The same flat listed on several sites becomes one card
• Every original listing stays one click away
• Price differences between sites at a glance

SPOT THE GOOD ONES FIRST
• "New" badge on listings that weren't there last time
• Price history for every listing, with price-drop badges and a price-drop filter
• Sort by newest, price, €/m², size or biggest price drop
• Free-text search across title, street and district

FILTERS THAT DO WHAT THEY SAY
• Region, down to single Vienna districts
• Rent, size, rooms, category and source
• You get exactly what you tick. Nothing hidden, nothing extra

SAVED SEARCHES AND FAVOURITES
• Keep as many searches as you like and switch between them instantly
• Favourites per search, with average rent, price per m², size and rent range of your shortlist

SMART EXTRAS (Chrome 138 and newer)
• Translate listing descriptions into your language, on your device
• AI location rating for public transport, everyday shopping and green space, done by the browser's built-in AI

MADE FOR DAILY USE
• Three looks: Styled, Modern and Compact
• Light and dark mode, English and German
• Full mobile layout with pull-to-refresh, for Android browsers that support extensions

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no tracking, no ads, no RentRadar server
• Searches, listings and price history are stored only in your browser
• Scans run only when you start them, never in the background
• No contact details are collected. You contact landlords through the original listing
• See how much space your data uses and clean it up in one click
